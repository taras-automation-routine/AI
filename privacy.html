Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. Taras IT ("we") runs taras-it.com. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you visit or use the contact form — no legal jargon, just the facts.

What we collect

If you fill in the contact form on the homepage, we ask for your name, a phone number or email address, your preferred contact method, and — if you write one — a short message about what you need. There are no hidden fields and we don't ask for anything else.

If you'd rather just call or email us directly using the links on the site, the only information involved is whatever you choose to say.

Why we collect it

Only to get back to you. Submitting the form sends your details straight to us so we can reply and set up a call — nothing is used for anything beyond following up about automation for your business.

How the form actually works

When you submit the form, your details are saved directly into a private database we use to track requests — they are not posted anywhere public, and the database isn't browsable or searchable by anyone outside our team. You can still reach us the old-fashioned way too, by calling or emailing the addresses shown on the site — those never touch the database at all.

How long we keep it

We keep your request for as long as we're in touch about your project, and for a reasonable time after in case you get back in touch — generally no more than a couple of years. After that, or sooner if you ask, we delete it.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent, or hand your information to anyone for marketing. A few outside services help run the site itself:

  • → Google Fonts loads the typefaces used on this page — Google may see the IP address making that request.
  • → Supabase hosts the database that stores form submissions on our behalf — it only stores the data, it doesn't use it for its own purposes.
  • → Our email provider delivers messages you send us directly, the same as any email.
  • → If we ever turn on visitor analytics, it will only run after you click "Accept" on the cookie banner — never before, and never for the "Only necessary" choice.

Cookies

The only thing we store in your browser right now is your cookie-banner choice (whether you picked "Accept" or "Only necessary"), saved locally on your device so we don't ask again. It isn't sent to us or anyone else.

How to ask us to delete your data

Email book@taras-it.com and ask us to delete your information — we'll remove it from our inbox and any notes we took, and confirm once it's done. You can also just clear your cookie choice by clearing your browser's site data for taras-it.com.

Questions

Reach out any time at book@taras-it.com or +1 347-722-1230, and we'll answer directly — no support queue.
